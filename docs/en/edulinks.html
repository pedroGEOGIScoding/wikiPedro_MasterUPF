1 Educational Links of Interest

1.1 🤖 Artificial Intelligence for Education

MagicSchool AI https://www.magicschool.ai/ Artificial intelligence platform for teachers; helps create automated educational resources and experiences, reducing teaching workload.

Google NotebookLM https://notebooklm.google.com/ Google Labs AI for note-taking, research, and organizing information with documents, visual summaries, and collaboration.

Lumivara https://lumivara.at/ Educational platform with AI that helps generate interactive materials, questionnaires and summaries.

Kimi-ppt (Kimi Slides) https://ppt.ai/es/kimi-ppt AI that creates automatic presentations from text makes it easy to quickly generate slides for teachers and students.

1.2 🔬 Natural Sciences - Simulators and Virtual Laboratories

PhET Interactive Simulations https://phet.colorado.edu/ Free and interactive simulators for physics, chemistry, biology and mathematics experiments developed by the University of Colorado Boulder.

ChemCollective https://chemcollective.org/ Virtual laboratories and guided activities to teach chemistry, with simulations and interactive exercises.

Algodoo https://www.algodoo.com/ 2D physics sandbox to experiment and simulate physical phenomena through visual and creative simulation, free.

Lab Simulations OER – Mines LibGuides https://libguides.mines.edu/oer/simulationslabs Guide to open virtual laboratories and simulations, useful for STEM teaching.

Ask A Biologist (ASU) https://askabiologist.asu.edu/ Portal where students and teachers can ask biology questions from experts, with activities and multimedia resources.

Biomolecules.cat http://biomolecules.cat/ Scientific and informative resources on biomolecules and molecular biology for teaching and dissemination.

1.3 🌍 Geology and Earth Sciences

Visible Geology https://www.visiblegeology.com/ Tool to create geological 3D diagrams and model visual and interactive structures.

Tectonic Explorer – Concord Consortium https://tectonic-explorer.concord.org/ Free simulator of tectonic processes to experiment with plates and rocks at an educational level.

Paleolatitude.org https://paleolatitude.org/#!/ Interactive calculator to visualize the paleogeographic latitude of any point on Earth.

AEPECT https://www.aepect.org/ Spanish Association for the Teaching of Earth Sciences, training and resources in geology.

CNIG Digital Photo Library https://fototeca.cnig.es/fototeca/ Digital repository to consult historical aerial photos of Spain, useful in geography and environmental studies.

1.4 🗺️ Mind Maps and Visual Organizers

XMind https://xmind.com/es/ Collaborative mind mapping and AI-assisted brainstorming tool, useful for organizing concepts and projects.

IHMC CmapTools https://cmap.ihmc.us/products/ Software for concept maps, allows you to create, share and edit knowledge and learning models.

CmapTools – IHMC https://cmap.ihmc.us/ Visual tool to create and share concept maps in science and education.

MindMup https://www.mindmup.com/ Online tool to create mind maps quickly and easily.

Instamaps https://www.instamaps.cat/#/ Free platform to create collaborative maps and interactive geographic visualizations.

1.5 📊 Interactive Tools for the Classroom

Wooclap Word Cloud Generator https://www.wooclap.com/en/word-cloud-generator/ Instant word cloud creation tool, useful for visual summary and group analysis in class.

Mentimeter: Event evaluation https://www.mentimeter.com/templates/p/speakers-evaluate-event Templates to rate speakers and events with dynamic and interactive presentations.

1.6 💻 Educational Programming and Technology

Scratch https://scratch.mit.edu/ Free visual programming platform where students and teachers create interactive games and animations.

1.7 🎓 Inquiry and STEM Methodologies

LabInquiry – IQS Investigation https://iqs.edu/es/investigacion/proyectos-investigacion/labinquiry-labs-for-inquiry-based-teaching-in-mathematics-and-beyond/ International project on inquiry and laboratory in mathematics and science using STEM methodology.

LabInquiry – UB Secondary https://www.ub.edu/labinquiry/categoria/secundaria/ Activities and didactic inquiry proposals for secondary school, in collaboration with the UB.

1.8 🎨 Concept Cartoons and Teaching Resources

Science Concept Cartoons – STEM Learning https://www.stem.org.uk/resources/library/collection/4226/science-concept-cartoons Educational comics that promote scientific discussion and identify misconceptions.

Concept Cartoons – Science Education Research https://science-education-research.com/teaching-science/concept-cartoons/ Resources and explanation on using vignettes to detect and address misconceptions in science.

Nature of Science Concept Cartoons – RSC Education https://edu.rsc.org/resources/the-nature-of-science-concept-cartoons/1277.article Cartoons that help debate and diagnose key ideas in natural sciences.

Science Concept Cartoons – Classroom Secrets https://classroomsecrets.co.uk/campaign/science-concept-cartoons Templates and resources to encourage scientific and critical thinking in the classroom.

1.9 📚 Academic Resources and Repositories

Learner.org https://www.learner.org/ Educational multimedia resources and activities for science, mathematics, humanities—ideal for teachers and students.

DDD UAB https://ddd.uab.cat/ Digital repository of the Autonomous University of Barcelona to consult and download scientific-academic production.

BibGuru https://www.bibguru.com/es/ Free bibliographic citation generator, compatible with APA, MLA and more, ideal for academic works.

UPF Master Wiki – PedroGeoGISCoding https://pedrogeogiscoding.github.io/wiki_MasterUPF/ Wiki with notes and resources on teaching geography, technology and GIS in the UPF master’s degree.

1.10 📋 Curriculum and Educational Regulations (Spain and Catalonia)

LOMLOE curricula by CCAA https://educagob.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/curriculo/curriculo-lomloe/curriculos-ccaa.html Current official educational curricula by Autonomous Community of Spain according to the LOMLOE law.

XTEC Basic Curriculum https://sites.google.com/xtec.cat/curriculumbasica/p%C3%A0gina-principal?authuser=0 Documentation and resources of the basic educational curriculum in Catalonia.

INTEF https://intef.es/ Institute dedicated to the training and updating of teachers in educational technology and teaching resources in Spain.

INEE https://www.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/inee/portada.html Spanish National Institute that carries out evaluations and studies on the educational system and academic performance.

ARC CESIRE XTEC https://serveiseducatius.xtec.cat/cesire/arc/ Portal of resources and innovative educational proposals of the CESIRE.

ARC applications XTEC https://apliense.xtec.cat/arc/ Portal of resources and active applications for learning, linked to CESIRE-XTEC.

1.11 🎯 Playful Resources and Gamification

Murdle https://murdle.com/ Logic and mystery game “murder mystery” with daily deductive logic challenges.

1.12 🔗 Institutional Access

UPF Authentication https://autenticacio.upf.edu/sso/SAML2/SSOService.php Secure login page for Pompeu Fabra University via SAML2, protected access to institutional resources.

1.13 📡 RSS Feeds and Aggregators

RSS Channel – Dynamic Resources https://rss.app/feed/tvjse9tysjnwS5tz/embed?tab=carousel RSS feed to dynamically display and integrate news and resources into educational sites or presentations.
